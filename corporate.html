Content

  • The name CampHer immediately communicates the brand’s identity and purpose. It is a play on the word “Camper,” with the addition of “Her” to directly reflect the brand’s target audience: solo female travelers. By splitting the word into “Camp” and “Her,” the name highlights both the activity, which is camping and vanlife, and the empowerment of women who pursue it independently. It’s simple, memorable, and meaningful, reinforcing the brand’s mission to provide safety, community, and confidence to women on the road.

    Design Elements

    Please provide a list of design elements alongside their justifications:

    • Colour scheme:
      • #8F7E62 The light brown signifies stability, reliability, and comfort. This light shade of brown gives a grounded, warm feeling that represents the softness of nature. It aligns with CampHer’s mission of offering a safe and cozy space for solo travelers. It subtly reassures users that they are supported and understood. Used in the logo for contrast and the purpose of bringing those feelings to the users.
      • #4C3921 The dark brown signifies strength and security. This deep, natural tone brings a sense of calm, confidence, and protection. It reinforces the safety and trust aspects of CampHer. Dark brown suggests a strong foundation, ideal for a platform helping women travel with confidence. Used for the logo, but also text on the website and social media posts to ensure maximum readability on light backgrounds. It is also softer on the eyes than black, reducing visual strain when reading while still maintaining strong contrast and making the brand look more consitent.
      • #F3B23F The mustard yellow signifies optimism, courage, and adventure. The color is bright, inviting and energetic without being loud. It evokes curiosity and gives a sense of sunshine, new roads, and joyful movements. This color captures the spirit of exploration and empowerment. It communicates enthusiasm and calls users to action—perfect for buttons and highlights, encouraging engagement while keeping the visual elements consistent.
      • #95A387 The green signifies trust, peace, and wellness. This soft green mimics the color of sage leaves and forest. It brings a sense of balance, serenity, and connection to nature. It aligns with the outdoor, vanlife lifestyle CampHer supports. Green reassures users that this is a calming, friendly environment, far from stress and noise. It frames the site as it is used on the header and footer strengthening this nature-forward identity across every page. The purpose of using it as a background color on the Safety Tips is to evoke trust and bring peace as it might be a stressful topic, so harsh colors are avoided. This color is also used for modal backgrounds to reduce decision-making anxiety and maintain a sense of tranquility. It makes these pop-ups feel like part of the environment rather than interruptions.
    • Font choices:

      The font used for the body text, buttons and descriptive content is Pangolin, a rounded, hand-drawn sans-serif that balances legibility with a warm, human touch. This typeface reinforces CampHer's approachable and community-driven identity. For solo female campers, it is important that tools and platforms feel personal and inviting. Pangolin's slightly playful curves and informal style create a sense of comfort, authenticity, and friendliness—all essential qualities for building trust and emotional safety. Its handwritten feel also complements CampHer's emphasis on community and shared experiences, making it ideal for a brand that prioritizes support, connection, and empowerment. Overall, Pangolin reflects CampHer's core values: safety, comfort, empowerment, and nature, wrapped in a warm and minimalistic visual language.

      Quicksand is used for CampHer’s headings and titles to create a clear visual hierarchy while maintaining a friendly and approachable tone. As a geometric sans-serif typeface, Quicksand offers excellent readability and a clean, modern appearance. Its rounded terminals and balanced proportions soften the boldness typically associated with headers, making it feel more welcoming and less formal. This aligns perfectly with CampHer’s brand values of accessibility, empowerment, and warmth. By pairing Quicksand with the handwritten Pangolin body text, the design achieves a thoughtful balance—combining clarity and structure with personality and friendliness. Overall, Quicksand supports a visual language that is both organized and approachable, ideal for a community-centered platform like CampHer.

      Caveat is used sparingly for quotes and emotional highlights in CampHer’s branding to add a layer of intimacy and authenticity. As a casual, cursive handwritten typeface, Caveat mimics natural handwriting, making messages feel more personal, almost as if they were written by a fellow traveler or a friend. This reinforces CampHer’s values of community, shared experiences, and emotional connection. The expressive strokes of Caveat create visual contrast against the more structured Quicksand and Pangolin fonts, drawing attention to key messages without overwhelming the overall design. Its use adds a human touch that aligns with the brand’s mission to create a safe, relatable, and empowering space for solo female campers.

    • User Interface Patterns:

      • The CampHer website is designed with a user-focused interface that supports its mission of empowering solo female travelers. The site’s layout and structure reflect the values of clarity, comfort, and community, offering visitors an intuitive and approachable digital experience. Built on Bootstrap’s responsive 12-column grid, the layout adjusts seamlessly across screen sizes. On larger screens, content is often arranged side by side in two or three columns, making efficient use of space while maintaining visual balance. On smaller devices like mobile phones, the columns stack vertically, preserving readability and function. This approach ensures a consistent and optimized experience no matter the device, enhancing usability for all visitors.

      The navigation bar remains fixed at the top, allowing users to easily jump between sections like “Experiences,” “Safety Tips,” and “Corporate.” It helps orient users and minimizes friction when browsing. The footer is made the same color as the navigation bar to create a sense of framing, offering social links and a clear brand message, which reinforces consistency across pages.

      Visual hierarchy is used throughout the site. Headings clearly separate content and help users identify key information at a glance. Elements such as buttons and interactive modals are used purposefully to encourage user action, whether it's joining the community or signing up for workshops. These components are strategically placed and styled to be inviting without being overwhelming.

      The homepage layout guides them through a logical and engaging journey. The hero section features a large welcoming message layered over an image of campervan window, immediately communicating the brand's target audience. Followed by clear sections that break down content into smaller sections, such as “About Us,” “Join Us,” and “Our Vision.” Each section is placed and designed for easy scanning, which helps maintain user interest and supports accessibility. This type of layout helps users naturally group related content and understand the hierarchy of information on the page. The "Join Us" section has a contrasting color background to grab the attention of the viewers and make a call to action. The images featured on the homepage of CampHer play a key role in visually reinforcing the brand's values and emotional tone. Each picture evokes a sense of freedom, adventure, and peaceful solitude. These visuals are not only aesthetically pleasing but also emotionally resonant, aligning with the lifestyle that CampHer encourages. Directly beneath the image carousel, the "Our Values" section clearly outlines the brand’s core principles: Safety, Freedom, Authenticity, and Empowered Community. These values are presented in simple, colorful blocks that are easy to scan and understand, giving users a quick yet meaningful insight into what the brand stands for. The use of icons and clean layout helps break up the text, keeping the sections visually light and accessible. Beneath the values, the quote "Empowering women to explore freely with safety, comfort, and confidence." serves as a mission statement. Positioned strategically before the footer, this line summarizes the emotional and practical support CampHer offers.

      The Experiences page of the CampHer website is designed to foster a sense of community and trust through storytelling. Its purpose is for users to share their solo travel experiences and give even more tips and tricks to others, making an endless list of safety tips and inspiration. Structurally, it uses a clear grid layout to present user stories in neatly spaced cards, making the content easy to navigate while maintaining visual harmony. On top of the page there is a picture of a group of campers, signifying community. Each card features a portrait or lifestyle photo, a name, age, and location, followed by a personal testimony from a solo female camper. The use of real stories strengthens authenticity and aligns with CampHer’s mission to create a supportive, empowering space. The page also includes a consistent call to action inviting users to comment or engage, reinforcing community interaction. At the bottom, a dedicated section allows visitors to “Share your story now,” further encouraging user participation.

      The Safety Tips page of the CampHer website serves as a practical and empowering resource for solo female travelers. It is structured to be clear, approachable, and confidence-boosting. The layout is organized in a user-friendly grid system, allowing tips to be visually separated into blocks. This enhances readability and ensures that users can quickly scan through the page and retain key information. At the top, the page features a campsite picture, and a clean and inviting headline followed by a brief italic subtext, setting a calm and encouraging tone. Each safety tip is presented with concise language and a consistent visual format, making the advice accessible to all levels of campers, from beginners to seasoned travelers. Next to each tip there is a picture, setting the tone for what is going to be talked about and also to make it more interesting, rather that just having dry text. The bottom of the page features an attention-grabbing picture and a call to action to join safety workshops and the community, encouraging users even further to engage.

      Finally, the site demonstrates responsiveness and accessibility. Touch-friendly buttons, readable layouts, and interactive modals are all considered in the design. The CampHer website is built on solid UI principles: consistent structure, clear layout, responsive design, and intuitive navigation. These elements combine to create a trustworthy and empowering space for solo female campers to feel supported, connected, and informed.

      Testing Report

      • The primary goal of our testing is to evaluate how effectively the website communicates its purpose and supports the needs of our target audience: solo female travelers interested in camper van adventures. We want to understand whether users can easily navigate the site and access important features such as safety information, campsite suggestions, and community spaces. Additionally, we aim to assess if the visual design and overall tone of the website create a sense of trust, empowerment, and comfort. Finally, we hope to identify any usability issues or moments of confusion that could restrict the overall user experience, so we can refine and improve the platform accordingly.
      • To evaluate the usability and visual coherence of our website prototype, we conducted a user testing session with four participants. Three of them aligned well with our target audience: independent, safety-conscious women interested in van travel. One participant, although male and therefore outside our core demographic, still provided valuable insights from a UX (user experience) perspective, which we found extremely helpful in refining functionality. The testing took place on campus in a casual, focused setting, where participants were asked to interact with our paper-based prototype of the Campher website. We provided clear instructions, asking them to share their thoughts and impressions out loud while exploring the site. We guided them to reflect on key aspects such as content clarity, navigation flow, accessibility, and visual appeal. These sessions were audio recorded to ensure we could review their feedback in detail later. Overall, the responses were very positive, confirming that our design direction resonated with users. However, one participant made a particularly insightful observation: she suggested that the navigation bar (originally designed as a hidden vertical menu) would be more intuitive and user-friendly if placed horizontally and kept visible at all times at the top of the page. We took this feedback seriously and implemented the change to improve the overall navigation experience. This testing phase confirmed that our website design is not only aligned with our brand’s values of safety, ease, and empowerment, but also responsive to the real needs of our users.

      Marketing

      Context of campaign and promotional activities

      The Campher campaign was designed to promote a safe, empowering space for solo female van campers. The message focused on normalizing solo travel for women, while emphasizing safety, preparation, and confidence. The aim was to engage and connect with Gen Z and Millennial women by using digital storytelling and community interaction across key platforms. Our promotional activities were primarily executed on Instagram and Facebook, based on audience research from our Communication Media Plan. These platforms were chosen because they are where our audience is most active and engaged, particularly when it comes to lifestyle, travel, and wellness content. The campaign included regular feed posts twice a week (Tuesdays and Fridays), and daily Instagram stories featuring behind-the-scenes content, safety tips, polls, and interactive elements. The objectives, as outlined in our Communication Media Plan, were to:

      • Reach at least 400 Instagram profiles;
      • Receive 20+ likes per post with a few meaningful comments;
      • Start building a loyal and interactive community;
      • Consistently post 2x per week and maintain daily story activity.

      Learning Points

      Throughout the project, we gained valuable insights into social media marketing, particularly within a niche audience. One of the key takeaways was that growing a following takes more time and strategy than expected. Even though we stayed consistent with posting and maintained strong visual branding, overall engagement (likes, comments, shares) was lower than we anticipated. On the positive side, our message and visual identity were clear and well-received. Users who came across our content understood Campher’s mission and appreciated the tone and purpose. However, it became clear that visibility alone is not enough, relying only on posts didn’t generate the reach or interaction we hoped for. If we were to do it again, we would combine posts with more active outreach, including influencer collaboration, paid ads, and more interactive formats. We also realized the importance of creating content that encourages users to participate, not just consume.

      Future Planning

      If the project were to continue, our next step would be to expand reach and boost engagement using a more strategic approach. Based on our experience and what we learned, we would:

      • Collaborate with influencers and vanlife communities to gain trust and improve visibility within our niche;
      • Use paid promotions on Instagram and Facebook to target a wider and more specific audience;
      • Shift content from purely informative to a more interactive mix including polls, quizzes, user-generated content, and safety-related challenges or prompts;
      • Create short-form video content (e.g. Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) to present safety tips in a fun, shareable, and relatable format;
      • Partner with outdoor brands (e.g. camping gear, travel safety companies) for cross-promotions and giveaways to increase interest and credibility.

      Professionalism

      All of our campaign content was developed with care to reflect Campher’s brand identity. The images used in feed posts and on the site are high-quality, consistent in tone, and visually aligned with our earthy, nature-focused palette. Our posts maintained strong readability and coherence, using fonts and layouts that matched the brand's friendly and professional voice.

The first version was based on our initial assumptions about the target group.After making the intial interviews, link to which can also be found in the Appendix, we adjusted the lean canvas to better match our audience and that is reflected in the second version. The third and final version of the Lean Canvas is based off of the site testing interviews, which can also be found in the appendix. During those interviews the participants were given tasks to go through the initial version of the website. Here you can find a picture of our final version.

      • Problem

        1. On the final Lean canvas, we present seven problems of our target audience. To begin with, our interviewees stated that there is not enough verified sources that show secure and well-reviewd camping sites. Currently, they try to find places like that by searching for reviews online and seeing the ratings. It helps sometimes but in cannot be trusted completely.
        2. Another issue for our target group is the lack of built-in, female-focused security tools or campground infrastructure. There are not enough camping spots provided with security, which raises concern about outside people coming to the camp. The lack of built-in security tools in the van itself is also a problem, as it makes it harder to secure the van by themselves. That problem also connects to the theft concerns and inadequate ventilation. In case the weather is too hot, there are concerns about leaving windows open at nigh, as fear of theft ad break-in arise.
        3. There were multiple complains about limited emergency communication options during travel. Sometimes, connectivity may be lost, which would be a big problem for the campers in case of an emergency and no emergency services being close enough. The lack of tracking devices that work in those cases seemed to be a big problem.
        4. Finally, all of those problems contribute to stress that is stopping the campers to enjoy the moment to the fullest.
      • Current alternatives for women interested in solo camping include blog posts, general camping apps, and social media communities. However, these sources often lack credibility, verified safety information, and a focus on the specific needs of female travelers. While platforms like Google Maps and national park websites do provide campsite details, they do not cater specifically to the concerns and safety priorities of solo female campers.

      Solution

      • We focused on providing foursolutions for our target group, the first one being acurated platform with a verified database of safe campsites, parking areas, and emergency services. That resolves the problem of not being able to trust sites like GoogleMaps and Blog posts.
      • Second of all, we decided to provide the users with a growing network where users can share real-time safety updates, ask questions, and receive expert advice from experienced solo travelers tailored for solo female travelers.
      • After conducting the initial interviews we found out that weapons and violence in case of a problem may backfire so we decided to give them uides on non-violent safety strategies like decoys, alarms, lighting etc.
      • Finally, we made a community chat to locate and connect with other female campers nearby, which resolves the fear of isolation and fear of unknown people at night.
